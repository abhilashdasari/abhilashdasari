Research journey

Research Experience

Student Exchange Program

IHU-ICAN, Sorbonne University, Paris, France

Feb 2025 - July 2025

Working with Dr. Maharaja Ponniah and Dr. Nadine Suffee on discovering novel biomarkers of atrial cardiomyopathy using imagery and tissue biology. Conducted high-resolution single-cell (scRNA-seq) and spatial transcriptomics processing, QC, normalization, and cell-type annotation, and integrated spatial data with tissue histology and imaging to map spatial niches, disease-specific cell states, and intercellular signaling pathways (CellChat).

PhD Thesis Project (Bioinformatics)

Indian Institute of Technology Delhi

September 2020 - October 2026

Working with Dr. Ishaan Gupta on dissecting the role of alternative splicing in respiratory and hepatobiliary pathology. Developed automated, reproducible end-to-end pipelines (R, Python, BASH) on Slurm-managed HPC environments for deep transcriptomic profiling, alternative splicing quantification, and differential expression across multi-cohort clinical datasets; performed multi-omics integration linking transcriptomic signatures with clinical metadata; architected a privacy-preserving, on-premise LLM inference stack (llama.cpp, Qwen 27B & 125B) for automated bioinformatics scripting; and collaborated with clinical and pathology teams at AIIMS.

University of Hyderabad

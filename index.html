A little about me

Curious about the biology behind the signal.

I’m a bioinformatician working across transcriptomics, genomics, and multi-omics. My research asks how changes in gene expression and RNA processing relate to disease, and how computational analysis can help make those patterns easier to interpret.

At IIT Delhi, my PhD work has focused on alternative splicing in respiratory and hepatobiliary pathology. I’ve built reproducible analysis workflows for clinical datasets and worked with collaborators across clinical and pathology teams. A research exchange at Sorbonne University brought me into single-cell and spatial transcriptomics for atrial cardiomyopathy.

Outside research, I enjoy running, swimming, and cycling.

What brings the work together

Moving between molecular measurements, computational methods, and the biological context they are meant to explain.
